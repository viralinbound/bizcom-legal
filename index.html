About Bizcom Legal

Your Victorian law experts.

Bizcom Legal is a Victorian law firm led by Principal Lynn Saldanha — admitted to practice as a Solicitor and Barrister in the Supreme Court in 2009. We advise on commercial law, property and conveyancing (VIC, NSW, QLD, WA & ACT), family law, dispute resolution and litigation (VCAT & the courts), and wills & estates.
