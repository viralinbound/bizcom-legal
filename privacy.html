Privacypolicy

How Bizcom Legal collects, uses, stores, secures and discloses personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). Last updated: 2026.

1. Overview2. Information we collect3. How we collect it4. Use & disclosure5. Security & storage6. Access & correction7. Website & cookies8. Overseas disclosure9. Complaints10. Contact us

1. Overview

Bizcom Legal (“we”, “us”, “our”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

By providing personal information to us — including through this website, by email, by telephone, or in person — you consent to it being handled as set out in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not provide personal information to us.

2. Information we collect

The personal information we collect depends on your dealings with us and may include:

  • Identity and contact details — name, email address, telephone and mobile numbers, and postal address;
  • Information relating to your legal matter and the instructions you give us;
  • Information you provide through our enquiry forms, including your preferred contact time and a description of your matter;
  • Records of correspondence and communications with us;
  • Technical information when you use our website (see “Website & cookies”).

Some matters may require us to collect sensitive information. Where we do, we will only collect it with your consent or where otherwise permitted by law, and we will handle it with additional care.

3. How we collect it

We collect personal information directly from you wherever practicable — for example when you contact us, complete an enquiry form, or instruct us on a matter. In some circumstances we may collect information from third parties (such as other parties to a transaction, government agencies, or courts and tribunals) where it is necessary for your matter and permitted by law. If we receive information about you that we did not request, we will deal with it in accordance with the APPs.

4. Use & disclosure

We use and disclose personal information for the purposes for which it was collected, including to:

  • Provide legal services and advice, and conduct your matter;
  • Respond to your enquiries and communicate with you;
  • Administer, manage and improve our services;
  • Meet our legal, regulatory and professional obligations.

We may disclose personal information to third parties where reasonably necessary for your matter — for example to courts and tribunals, other parties and their representatives, barristers and experts, government and regulatory bodies, and our service providers — or where required or authorised by law. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for direct marketing without your consent.

5. Security & storage

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference and loss, and from unauthorised access, modification or disclosure. This website is served over a secure encrypted connection (HTTPS). We maintain physical, electronic and procedural safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information we hold.

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by law and our professional obligations. When information is no longer required, we take reasonable steps to securely destroy or de-identify it.

6. Access & correction

You may request access to the personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it if it is inaccurate, out of date, incomplete, irrelevant or misleading. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We will respond within a reasonable period. There may be circumstances where we are unable to provide access or make a correction — for example where doing so would be unlawful or affect another person’s privacy — and if so we will explain why in writing.

7. Website & cookies

When you visit our website, our server and any analytics tools we use may record limited technical information such as your device and browser type, pages visited and the date and time of access. This information is generally used in aggregate to maintain and improve the website. Our website may use cookies; you can configure your browser to refuse cookies, although some features may not function as intended. Our website may contain links to third-party sites that are not governed by this policy.

8. Overseas disclosure

We primarily store and handle personal information in Australia. Some service providers we use (for example secure cloud or email providers) may store information outside Australia. Where personal information is disclosed to an overseas recipient, we take reasonable steps to ensure it is handled consistently with the APPs.

9. Complaints

If you believe we have breached the Australian Privacy Principles or mishandled your personal information, please contact us in the first instance using the details below. We will investigate and respond to your complaint within a reasonable period.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC):

  • Website: www.oaic.gov.au
  • Phone: 1300 363 992
